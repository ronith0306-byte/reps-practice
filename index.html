Freeze drill

Get past the blank moment in a casual chat.

A coworker line pops up. You get a 3 second countdown, then 30 seconds to answer. Open with a pause line or echo their last word.

Explain it

Explain something out loud, headline first.

One sentence version, then one detail, then an example. If a word goes missing, describe it and keep going. 60 seconds.

Taboo word

Describe a word without saying it.

This trains the exact move you need when a word disappears mid-sentence: talk around it. 45 seconds, no stopping.
